1. Executive Summary
The company is a small performance-publishing operation. It does not own a dating product. It owns audiences, original content, a public comparison/editorial website, first-party
attribution, affiliate relationships, and the decision logic that determines which approved offer receives traffic.

The first geographic market is the United Kingdom because Facebook still has very broad adult reach there, English lets the team quality-control AI-assisted storytelling directly,
and the UK has meaningful online-dating usage across adult age groups. The launch deliberately focuses on 30+ audiences rather than young mass-market traffic.

Core flywheel: original content -> organic reach -> followers -> repeat reach -> owned website -> partner click -> approved PPS sale -> revenue -> selective reinvestment into
proven content -> more audience.

2. Why This Model Is Worth Testing in the UK
Research supports the channel and audience choice, but no source can guarantee that an individual content idea will go viral.

• Facebook remains a major UK adult platform: Meta advertising data reported by DataReportal indicated about 38.8M Facebook users in the UK in late 2025 and reach
equivalent to about 69.9% of UK adults 18+.

• Facebook is still a daily habit: Ofcom's 2025 online report described Facebook among the UK's most commonly used apps.

• Older dating usage matters: Ofcom's 2024 dating analysis found Plenty of Fish and Match skewed strongly to ages 35-54, and users aged 55-64 who did use dating services
spent the most time on them among the reported age groups.

• Originality matters: Meta said views and watch time for original Facebook Reels approximately doubled in the second half of 2025 compared with the same period in 2024,
while duplicated or low-value edited content is deprioritised.

• Affiliate disclosure matters: UK ASA/CAP guidance treats affiliate marketing as advertising and requires commercial content to be obviously identifiable as advertising.

         Interpretation                           These signals justify testing a UK 30+ Facebook publishing network. They do not justify assuming a specific persona will monetise.
                                                  Month 1 is designed to create our own evidence.

3. Business Model and Economics
Revenue model: initially PPS/CPA only. The business should prefer clear first-sale economics before testing revenue-share. Every offer is evaluated by actual EPC and
approved revenue, not by headline payout alone.

Core commercial math:

       Affiliate EPC = approved affiliate revenue / partner clicks

       Paid-customer CPA = paid amplification spend / approved paying customers

       Organic revenue efficiency = approved affiliate revenue / organic reach x 1,000
A lower-payout offer can be more profitable if it converts better. Example: an offer paying GBP70 at 6% sale rate produces GBP4.20 expected revenue per partner click; a
GBP120 offer converting at 2% produces GBP2.40. The business routes more traffic to the stronger economics, not the bigger headline payout.

Monetisation rule for month 1: Do not turn every post into an affiliate post. Weeks 1-2 contain no direct monetisation. Weeks 3-4 may use a small number of editorial guide /
comparison bridges after the audience has received substantial native value.

9. KPI System and Winner Logic
      Metric                                         Formula / meaning                              Why it matters

      Organic reach                                  Native reach reported by Page/post             Distribution.

      Shares / 1K reach                              shares / reach x 1,000                         How strongly people pass the idea to others.

      Comments / 1K reach                            comments / reach x 1,000                       Conversation strength.

      Follows / 1K reach                             new follows attributed / reach x 1,000         Audience-building efficiency.

      Website visits / 1K reach                      site sessions from page/post / reach x 1,000   Commercial intent.

      Partner CTR                                    partner clicks / comparison visitors           How persuasive/relevant the comparison is.

      PPS conversion                                 approved sales / partner clicks                Offer fit.

      EPC                                            approved affiliate revenue / partner clicks    Offer economics.

      Revenue / 1K organic reach                     approved revenue / organic reach x 1,000       Ultimate organic media value.



Initial organic winner rule: flag a post if reach is at least 2.0x the Page's rolling median OR shares per 1,000 reach are at least 1.5x the Page median OR Reel watch
performance materially exceeds that Page's baseline. AudienceOS should make thresholds configurable after the first 50-100 posts.

What happens after a winner: extract the underlying insight (for example, independence after divorce), then create 3-5 new executions using a different situation, hook and
visual. Never duplicate the winner word-for-word.
