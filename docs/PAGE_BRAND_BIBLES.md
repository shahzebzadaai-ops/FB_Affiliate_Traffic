# Page brand bibles

The five launch Pages are data in `src/domain/pages.ts`. Do not publish the same creative on more than one Page. Fictional stories must read as editorial, not as real testimonials. Use natural UK English.

| Code | Page | Audience | Tone |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| BGUK | Back in the Game UK | Divorced or separated adults, 35–50 | Warm, hopeful, lightly funny, never pitying |
| AHSUK | After Hours Singles UK | Busy professionals, 30–45 | Dry British humour, modern, never corporate |
| SPSUK | Single Parent Stories UK | Single mums and dads, 30–50 | Supportive, practical, no identifiable children |
| SGIUK | Still Got It UK | Mature singles, 50+ | Premium and confident, never patronising |
| CBCUK | Casual By Choice UK | Men 30–50 who want low-pressure dating | Direct and respectful, never explicit or misogynistic |

Each Page record includes the bio, persona, visual direction, pillars, hook guidelines, templates and weekly format rhythm. The in-app brand bible is the staff-facing copy of this table.

Visual systems stay distinct: cream and navy, graphite city evenings, soft blue family-life imagery, navy and cream maturity, and dark navy male lifestyle. No purple gradients and no sexualised bait.
