# Integration roadmap

Nothing in this list is connected.

Google Drive: OAuth for the company account, without sharing Shahzeb's password. Store folder and file IDs. Phase 1 only shows the planned path and the folder names.

Meta: official Page permissions for publishing and insights, when approved. Until then, Masho publishes manually and pastes the public URL. Organic and paid metrics are typed in at 24 hours, 72 hours and 7 days. The platform cannot evade Meta's rules, and Phase 1 does not publish.

Affiliate networks: offers, countries, traffic-source permission, PPS amounts, conversion rules, network click IDs, first-party click IDs, subIDs, postback endpoints, approved and rejected conversions, refunds, payouts, and crypto withdrawal records where a network supports them. Postbacks need signature checks and idempotency. No secret is stored in the frontend.

AI providers may be added later. Prompts stay editable by a person.

The public comparison site is a separate product and is not in this app.
