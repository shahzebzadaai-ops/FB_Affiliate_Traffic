# Product master

Affiliate Traffic OS runs the company. It does not run a dating product.

Business flow:

Original Facebook content → organic reach and followers → editorial or comparison website → affiliate partner click → dating advertiser website → first paying customer → approved PPS commission → company revenue.

PPS means pay per sale: payment for a qualifying customer or sale. Partners own their products, payments, support, subscriptions and retention.

The company owns audience research, content production, Page growth, organic traffic, original Reels and images, assignments, approvals, publishing, community engagement, performance analysis, comparison websites, offer selection, click attribution, postbacks and confirmed commissions.

Paid advertising may support a post after it has proved itself. It does not replace organic distribution.

APPROVED OPERATING MODEL. Masho is the Facebook Page operations employee. He is not the creative producer. He does not write prompts, scripts, images or Reels. The AI pipeline prepares research, hooks, stories, captions, images, Reel scripts, voiceovers, subtitles and finished downloadable files. Shahzeb approves strategy, offers, tracking, money, staff roles, AI spend, new Pages and new countries. Fictional stories are editorial scenarios, not testimonials. Nothing publishes, spends or creates a Page without his explicit permission.

The first configuration is the United Kingdom, dating affiliate publishing, five Facebook Pages, Shahzeb as Super Admin and Masho as the page operator. Countries, staff, Pages, websites, networks and verticals are records, not hardcoded exceptions.

The 180-post calendar and permanent content IDs stay. Finished assets are produced on a rolling seven-day pipeline, not all at once. That pipeline is not connected yet. See `BUILD_STATUS.md`.
