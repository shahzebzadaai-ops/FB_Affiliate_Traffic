# Business model

Revenue is approved PPS commission. A headline payout is not the decision. Route traffic by actual earnings per click and approved revenue.

Month 1 is a 30-day validation sprint, not a scale sprint. Weeks 1 and 2 have no affiliate selling. Weeks 3 and 4 may use a small number of editorial guide bridges, and only with a clear advertising disclosure.

Do not turn every post into an affiliate post. Do not buy followers. Do not rescue a weak post with paid spend. Paid support, if used after day 15, amplifies posts that have already beaten that Page's own baseline.

The public comparison site is a separate product. The PDF suggests the working name DateRoute UK, which still needs a name-availability check. It is not built in Phase 1.

Attribution chain to preserve:

Page → post → website visitor → comparison page → affiliate click → network postback → approved PPS → revenue.

Every post keeps a permanent content ID, for example `UK-BGUK-001`. The PDF row ID `BGUK-001` remains the source ID.

Organic reach and paid reach are stored separately. A post is not a proven winner from a small sample. Recommendations stay subject to Shahzeb's approval.
