# Staff workflow

Masho opens the preview and sees the UK launch day, today's assigned tasks, what is still to produce, what is waiting for approval, what is overdue, the next deadlines and his Pages.

Start task opens the production workspace. It answers what to make, which Page, who it is for, the angle, the hook, what to paste into ChatGPT, which images and scenes to make, duration, voiceover, subtitles, thumbnail, caption, where files should go, what Shahzeb checks, when to submit, when to publish, where to paste the Facebook URL, and when to record performance.

Reel, image, poll and carousel checklists are different. Evidence is required before submission. Version notes are kept when work is submitted, changed, approved, postponed, cancelled, published or measured.

Shahzeb reviews submissions, requests changes, approves, moves a deadline or cancels a post. Training is seven lessons and can be reused for a future operator. Completion in Phase 1 is stored in the browser preview, not in a staff account.

Google Drive folders are planned, not connected:

`Affiliate Traffic OS/UK/{Page name}/{YYYY-MM}/{content ID}/`

Inside that folder: Brief, Scripts, Images, Audio, Working-Videos, Final-Videos, Thumbnails, Captions.
