# Staff workflow

Masho opens the app and sees his Pages and the publishing queue. Ready to publish stays at zero until a real file exists. He does not choose a topic or write a prompt.

A publishing task shows the Page, the UK slot, the finished file when one exists, the final caption, the thumbnail, manual Meta Business Suite instructions, download when a file exists, a quality flag, and the Facebook URL. If the asset is unsuitable he flags it for regeneration. That flag does not start an AI job and does not spend money.

He cannot see affiliate offers, tracking links, postbacks, revenue or AI spend. He cannot create a Page or change strategy.

Shahzeb reviews strategy and AI proposals, approves spend and expansion, and can still open the creative brief. Training now teaches review, flagging, manual scheduling and recording real numbers. Completion is stored on the staff account.

Google Drive folders are planned, not connected:

`Affiliate Traffic OS/UK/{Page name}/{YYYY-MM}/{content ID}/`

Inside that folder: Brief, Scripts, Images, Audio, Working-Videos, Final-Videos, Thumbnails, Captions.
