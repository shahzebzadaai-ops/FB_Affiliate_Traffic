# Mandala strategy

The Mandala is the owner's map from daily work to one business outcome.

Central goal: $25,000 USD per month in repeatable approved affiliate revenue. The first genuine UK dating PPS sale before 31 December 2026 is an aspiration, not a promise.

Structure: one goal, eight pillars, eight actions each. Sixty-four actions. The seed is in `src/domain/mandala.ts` and the `mandala_*` tables.

Status values: NOT_STARTED, PLANNED, IN_PROGRESS, BLOCKED, AWAITING_EVIDENCE, NEEDS_REVIEW, COMPLETED, PAUSED.

Every seeded action is NOT_STARTED. Latest measurement is empty. Evidence is empty. The screen says awaiting first data. It does not show a percentage of revenue.

A completed checkbox is not a verified business result. Milestone B is where evidence, blockers and review dates become editable.

Milestones M0–M11 from the company goal are recorded here as the scale. They are not marked achieved.

- M0 infrastructure ready: in progress. Local auth exists. Production hosting does not.
- M1–M11: not started. No Pages are verified as launched. No approved revenue exists.
