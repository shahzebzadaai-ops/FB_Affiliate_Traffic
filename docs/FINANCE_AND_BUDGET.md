# Finance and budget

Owner-only. Masho receives 403 from `/api/finance` and cannot open the finance screen.

No budget amount is approved. Do not invent one. No expense rows exist yet. Categories for later entry include hosting, domains, AI tools, paid media, production, salary, contractors and software.

Revenue, received cash, costs, margin and runway stay empty until entered or verified. Unapproved conversions, gross sales, reversals and screenshots are not approved revenue or settled cash.

Exchange rates, when used later, need a timestamp. This module is not a tax-accounting package.

Milestone B adds expense entry. The current screen remains the not-connected placeholder.
