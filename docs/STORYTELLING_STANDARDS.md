# Storytelling standards

Original storytelling is the product. Do not assemble unrelated stock into a faceless video and call it a brand film.

A story needs an audience insight, hypothesis, hook, character, conflict, turn and an honest question. Invented scenes must not be presented as customer testimonials.

Quality bar: a readable first two seconds, clear sound, natural British narration, consistent character, correct dimensions, no watermark, no stolen asset, no fake comments, no explicit bait, no unsupported performance claim.

Reels, image cards, polls and carousels keep separate recipes. The task workspace already carries the Page, hook, scenes, prompts and checklist. Poll options and carousel slides missing from the PDF stay unresolved until Shahzeb approves them.
