# Brand architecture

| Level | Name | Usage | Identity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Parent organisation | AffiGrove Media Group | Formal introduction, documents, footer | Full signature; corporate green |
| Primary brand | AffiGrove | Navigation, everyday communication | Horizontal logo or wordmark |
| Internal platform | AffiGrove OS | Private sign-in, dashboard, browser title | Shared G; ink/sage app icon; OS lockup |
| Production engine | Money Printer Turbo | Internal product title only | Amber content-stack module icon; Source Sans 3 |
| Workforce module | Operator OS | Workforce area within AffiGrove OS | Slate-blue roster module icon; Source Sans 3 |

Money Printer Turbo remains the supplied internal name. In corporate communication, describe the capability as “AI-assisted content production” rather than foregrounding a name that can imply effortless profit. Do not rename code, database tables, integrations or routes.

The G belongs to the parent and the platform. Modules use the supplied task-based pictograms, never recoloured parent logos. Use a maximum of one module accent in a local header or selected navigation item. Module accents are not status indicators; state always has an explicit text label. In dark mode use the pale info/warning surface with its dark text, or neutral module labels; do not use dark amber or blue as small text on a dark surface.

All products share fonts, spacing, focus treatment, button shapes and neutral tokens. The corporate website uses expressive serif headlines and larger editorial spacing. OS uses sans-serif headings, compact rows and a darker sidebar. Preserve established application structure. Sub-brand icons do not appear in corporate navigation unless a product becomes an approved public offering.

Browser titles: “AffiGrove Media Group” for the corporate home; “[Page] | AffiGrove” for other corporate pages; “[Screen] | AffiGrove OS” internally. Do not expose employee or sensitive record information in public metadata.
