Status: first complete identity for owner review. The filename “Official” is the requested delivery name, not evidence of approval. Date: 15 October 2026.

## Positioning

AffiGrove is an audience-led media and technology company. It combines audience research, original production, publishing and performance marketing with proprietary tools. It should earn trust through the quality of its work and the clarity of its commercial relationships.

Brand idea: **Built around audiences.** This is a proposed positioning line, not a claim of operating results. Mission: build useful media and sustainable businesses by understanding audiences, creating original work and learning from verified outcomes. “Sustainable” means durable operating value; do not imply environmental certification.

The parent brand is broad enough for new markets and categories. The first commercial channel does not define the whole company. Avoid public “money machine” messaging, exaggerated revenue claims, invented partner logos and claims about company scale.

## Character and language

Intelligent: explain the work clearly. Credible: publish evidence, not aspiration as fact. Progressive: use technology with judgement. Precise: give concrete descriptions. Creative: find an editorial point of view. Confident: short direct sentences. Use UK English. Say “original content and considered growth”, not “unstoppable automated profit”.

Describe affiliate relationships transparently where relevant. The corporate site explains the company; editorial sites must carry their own appropriate commercial disclosures. Final legal copy must use approved company facts. Do not invent a registration number, address or email.

Selected: C, the Grove Loop, paired with the refined wordmark from A. An open, squared G has a clipped shoulder and an inward return. It communicates a recurring operating cycle without a tree, leaf, money symbol, arrow or chart. Its construction is original to this package, but no legal trademark search or guarantee of worldwide uniqueness has been performed.
