# Logo use

Masters are real vector paths. No fonts or raster images are required to render them. Use SVG on the web and the supplied transparent PNG at 2x for systems that do not accept SVG. Do not redraw or retype.

Primary horizontal canvas: 480 x 112; Media Group canvas: 480 x 132; OS canvas: 580 x 112. Visible horizontal symbol: 72 units high. Canonical standalone symbol: 64 x 64; principal band: 16 units; clipped shoulder: 16 x 16. The inward return is deliberately heavier than the open channel.

Let D be the visible symbol height. Keep at least D/4 external clear space around the visible artwork, and D/2 when placed beside other brands. Embedded SVG padding does not replace required clear space. Keep the primary lockup's fixed 24-unit symbol-to-wordmark gap. Wordmark-only clear space: half the capital A height.

Minimum widths: horizontal 144px, wordmark 100px, Media Group 320px, OS 200px. Regular standalone symbol minimum 24px. Use the dedicated hinted micro-icon at 16px. Default header logo: 216px desktop / 176px mobile. On small mobile headers use AffiGrove, not the full Media Group descriptor. Print: horizontal 30mm; full signature 65mm; symbol 6mm. These are practical starting limits; proof the actual process.

Approved: green or ink on white/paper/mist; white or paper on green; white or sage on dark ink/night; black on white for one-colour reproduction. Avoid green on ink or green on green. White assets are genuinely transparent and appear blank on white until placed on a dark background.

Never stretch, rotate, add outlines/shadows/gradients, separate the dot, change letter spacing, recolour individual letters, fill the open channel, or place on a busy image. Background artwork may crop or repeat the symbol; the actual logo must remain whole with clear space. Do not animate the logo in a way that harms recognition. Do not treat the construction grid as a pattern to be added around every logo.
